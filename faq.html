Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your nail appointment.

How long does a nail appointment take?

An average nail appointment takes between 1 and 3 hours, depending on the service and complexity of the nail art.

Do I need an appointment or do you accept walk-ins?

Appointments are required because I am currently a nail technician student.

How long do different nail services take?
  • Basic manicure: Approximately 1 hour
  • Gel manicure: Approximately 1 hour
  • Acrylic full set: 1.5 hours or longer
  • Acrylic fill: 1.5 hours or longer
  • Gel-X extensions: 1.5 hours or longer

Appointment times may vary depending on the nail art.

What types of nail art do you offer?

After graduation, I plan to offer a three-tier nail art system.

  • Tier 1: Solid colors, basic designs, French tips, and cat-eye polish.
  • Tier 2: Mix-and-match nail art, charms, ombré, and chrome isolation.
  • Tier 3: Detailed designs, 3D sculpting (my specialty), and junk nails.

I do not offer character artwork.

How should I prepare for my nail appointment?

I recommend a consultation through direct message or phone call, especially for first-time clients. During the consultation, we will discuss nail health, design inspiration, and any products currently on your nails.

I do not offer fills or polish changes on another nail technician's work.

What should I do if a nail chips or breaks after my appointment?

I plan to offer a two-week guarantee on nail extension services. If a nail breaks or becomes damaged, contact me and I will do my best to schedule a repair.

Basic and gel manicures are excluded from this guarantee.

How do you clean and sanitize your tools?

I am Barbicide certified and follow state board requirements for sanitation and disinfection. Disposable soiled tools are discarded, and reusable tools are thoroughly cleaned.

All metal implements undergo high-temperature sterilization for at least 60 minutes at 170 degrees Celsius.

My Barbicide certification and school ID will be on display unless I am under the supervision of an instructor.
